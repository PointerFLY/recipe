A tiny game of big ideas

  1. Pick your hand, then tap Buddy's hand or your other hand.
  2. Add the fingers together. At 5 or more, the tapped hand rests.
  3. One resting hand? Split 2, 3 or 4 fingers to wake it up.
  4. Get both of Buddy's hands resting to win!

Perfect play can repeat indefinitely.
